---
title: Wendmark for Chrome
description: A free browser extension that points at each step of a guide someone shared with you, while you do the clicking. Record and share your own guides too, no account needed.
---

Someone sent you a Wendmark link: a few steps to do something in a web app.
With the extension, a cursor points at each step **in the app itself**, in
your own signed-in session, and moves on when you have done it. You do every
click. Wendmark only ever shows the way.

You can record and share guides of your own the same way: click the icon on a
page, press **Record a process**, click through it, and copy a link. No
account, no server.

## Install it

The extension is on its way to the Chrome Web Store, where installing it will
take one click. Until then, it is installed from its source:

1. Get the code and build it (you need [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 22 and
   [pnpm](https://pnpm.io)):

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Wendmark/wendmark.git
   cd wendmark
   pnpm install
   pnpm --filter @wendmark/extension build
   ```

2. In Chrome, open `chrome://extensions`, switch on **Developer mode** (top
   right), press **Load unpacked** and choose `apps/extension/dist`.
3. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu so the Wendmark icon is always there.

Then go back to the tab with the link: **Start** appears there.

It works in Chrome and in other browsers built on Chromium, such as Edge and
Brave. Firefox and Safari are not supported yet.

## What it can and cannot see

- **Nothing, until you ask.** It runs on a page only when you click its icon,
  or when you start a guide and allow that one site. It never asks for "all
  websites".
- **Never what you type.** Guides are made of what was clicked, never of
  anything typed into a field.
- **Your guides stay in your browser.** A shared guide travels inside its
  link, after the `#`, which is never sent to any server, wendmark.dev
  included. Before sharing, you see exactly what goes, with the words from
  the page taken out by default.

## If a link will not open

Some companies block newly registered websites, and wendmark.dev is new. The
guide is inside the link itself, so you do not need the website: open the
Wendmark side panel from its icon, choose **Import a guide → Paste a link**,
paste it, and press **Play**.
