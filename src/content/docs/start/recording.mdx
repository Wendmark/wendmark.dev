A process is recorded where its buttons are, in your own app, and started from
the portal, where you are signed in. Then you tidy it up in the portal and
publish it. Nobody writes a selector.

This page assumes the snippet is on your app and the app is set up in the
portal. If not, start with [Install it](/start/install/). To watch the whole
thing first, see the [demo](/demo/record/).

## 1. Start from the portal

Open your app's page in the portal and press **Record a process**. Your app
opens in a new tab with the recorder in its corner.

<Shot
  name="portal-app"
  address="portal.example"
  alt="The Fika app's page in the Wendmark portal, with its processes listed and the Record a process button."
  caption="An app's page in the portal. Record a process is where it starts."
/>

No secret travels in the address. The new tab asks the portal for permission,
and the portal grants it because you, signed in, pressed the button. [How the
pairing works](/built/author-mode/), and why it is built that way.

## 2. Click through it

<Shot
  name="recorder-steps"
  closeUp
  alt="Wendmark's dark recorder panel after four clicks in Fika, listing four steps: Go to Subscription, Click Change plan, Choose Four bags a month and Click Save plan, with Pause, Add a note, Test and Save underneath."
  caption="After four clicks: four steps. The name goes in the recorder's header; Save sends a draft to the portal."
/>

Use your app as you normally would. Every click becomes a step, worded from
what you touched: "Go to Subscription", "Click Save plan". The wording is a
first draft; you will say it better in the next step.

While you record:

- **Pause** when you need to set something up that is not part of the
  process.
- **Add a note** for something the user should read rather than click.
- **Test** plays what you have so far, so you see what your users will see.
- **Fold it away.** Press the recorder's dark bar to shrink it into a pill
  when it covers what you need to click, and drag it by its header or its bar
  anywhere on the screen.
- **✕** asks before throwing anything away.

Name the process in the recorder's header and press **Save**. It arrives in
the portal as a draft. Nobody sees a draft.

:::note[What it never records]
The recorder reads structure and the labels your designers wrote: roles,
accessible names, test ids, selectors. It never reads what anyone types,
chooses or ticks. [The full trust model](/running/security/).
:::

## 3. Reword it, then publish

<Shot
  name="editing-reword"
  closeUp
  alt="A step in the portal being reworded: the text box says Open Subscription in the top menu, with Save and Cancel buttons."
  caption="Rewording a step in your own voice."
/>

Open the draft in the portal. For each step you can:

- **Reword** it in your product's own voice. What the user reads is yours to
  write.
- **Their own data**: mark a step that points at one of the user's own items
  (an order, a row, a coffee) rather than at fixed parts of the app. The guide
  then rings every similar item and lets the user pick theirs.
- **Move** it up or down, or **remove** a stray click.
- **Add something to read here** between steps.

Under **Where it appears**, list the pages it belongs to, as paths like
`/subscription`, so the same process works on staging and in production; and
choose whether it is offered in the launcher.

<Shot
  name="editing-steps"
  closeUp
  alt="The four steps of Change your plan in the portal after rewording, each with Reword, Their own data, move and Remove controls."
  caption="The steps after rewording, ready to publish."
/>

**Publish** freezes a version. You can keep editing the draft afterwards, and
nobody who is halfway through the process is disturbed until you publish again.

## 4. Follow it as a user would

Open your app normally, not from the portal, and find it under **Show me
how**, or call `Wendmark.start(id)` from your own help menu. It plays the way
your users will see it.
