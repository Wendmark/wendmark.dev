The customer's side of the process recorded in [Record and
publish](/demo/record/). This is a fresh browser that has never been signed in
to anything, visiting Fika.

<Clip
  name="guiding"
  alt="A visitor to Fika presses Show me how. The view zooms in on the list of processes, and they pick Change your plan. Back at full size, Wendmark's white teaching cursor glides to Subscription and rings it, and the visitor's own black pointer follows and clicks. What to watch:

- **Two pointers.** The white one with the teal ring is Wendmark showing the
  way. The black one is the person. Only the black one ever clicks.
- **The guide moves on because they did the step,** not because of a Next
  button. If they click something else, nothing breaks; the app behaves as
  it always does.
- **The panel says where they are** and what is left. It folds into a pill or
  drags aside whenever it is in the way, and ✕ ends the guide.
